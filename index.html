What is the difference between SEO, AEO, and GEO?
SEO (Search Engine Optimization) gets you ranked in traditional Google/Bing results, the 10 blue links. AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) targets zero-click surfaces: featured snippets, voice assistants, and direct answer boxes, where the goal is being the answer, not just a link. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) goes further, structuring content so generative AI systems like ChatGPT and Perplexity can find, understand, and cite it when synthesizing an answer from multiple sources. I treat all three as one connected system rather than separate services, since they overlap more than they compete.
What is SXO, and how is it different from CRO?
SXO (Search Experience Optimization) connects SEO and UX. It's not just about ranking, but about what happens the moment someone actually lands on the page: load speed, content clarity, and a clear path to the next step. CRO is narrower: testing and refining specific elements (headlines, CTAs, forms) to lift conversion rate. I pair the two rather than treating them as separate line items, since search intent, on-page experience, and conversion path are one continuous flow, not three unrelated steps.
